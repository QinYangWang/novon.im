This is a post. It is an `.mdx` file in `content/`, and its filename is its URL.

```text
content/hello-world.mdx  →  /hello-world
```

## Writing

Frontmatter at the top of the file drives everything:

```yaml
---
title: Hello, world
description: Shown in the post list and in feeds.
date: 2026-01-01
author: Your Name
---
```

Posts are sorted newest first on the home page and on `/blog`. Pages without a
`date` still appear, sorted after the dated ones.

## Adding a post

Create a new file, add frontmatter, save. That is the whole workflow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the file">
    `content/my-new-post.mdx`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add frontmatter">
    At minimum a `title`. Add `date` so the post sorts correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    Markdown, plus callouts, tabs, steps and cards without any imports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  `novon dev` reloads as soon as you save. `novon build` writes the static
  version, including `rss.xml` and `sitemap.xml`.
</Note>
